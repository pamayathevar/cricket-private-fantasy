# Cricket Private Fantasy League — User Guide

This guide explains how to build, submit, and follow a fantasy XI. League settings can vary by season or phase, so the live values shown in **League** and **Rules** are authoritative.

## Quick start

1. Open **League** and choose an upcoming fixture.
2. Select 11 players while following the displayed budget, role, and team limits.
3. Tap a selected player to set Captain, Vice Captain, or an impact role. Add a booster if desired and available.
4. Review the transfer count, then select **Submit XI** before the fixture locks.

## Navigation

- **League** — choose a fixture, build the Selected XI, assign match roles, and submit.
- **Ranking** — view overall and phase standings.
- **Results** — review submitted or locked lineups and published points.
- **Fixtures** — see match dates, lock status, and links to the League sheet or Results.
- **Player Pool** — inspect available players and their status.
- **Owner Squads** — review ownership, auction squads, and special-player selections.
- **Rules** — see the live league format, lineup rules, scoring, phases, and transfer periods.
- **Help** — open the in-app version of this guide.

On mobile, Player Pool, Owner Squads, Help, and Rules are available under **More**.

## Build the Selected XI

The Selected XI is the match sheet for the active fixture.

- Add players until the sheet shows **11/11**.
- Watch the validation messages for budget, minimum roles, maximum players from one team, and other league-specific conditions.
- Tap a selected player's details to edit their match roles.
- Select **×** only when you want to remove that player.
- Use the summary to review total cost, ownership mix, role mix, and transfer counts.

## Match roles

- **C (Captain)** — multiplies the player's full score. The usual multiplier is 2×.
- **VC (Vice Captain)** — multiplies the player's full score. The usual multiplier is 1.5×.
- **BAI (Batting Impact)** — doubles batting points only.
- **BOI (Bowling Impact)** — doubles bowling points only.

C or VC cannot be combined with BAI or BOI on the same player. Always check **Rules** for the multipliers used by the active league.

## Transfers

- **Match Transfers** compares the current XI with the previous submitted XI.
- **Period Transfers** shows transfers used against the allowance for the current transfer period.
- Using one of your own players is not charged as a transfer.
- The first submitted XI in a transfer period may be free if that setting is enabled.
- **SUP-TR (Super Transfer)** removes the transfer limit for one match.

Review the numbers shown beside the submission action before saving. They reflect the active fixture and league configuration.

## Submit, edit, and resubmit

### First submission

When the XI is valid, select **Submit XI**. A **SAVED** status confirms that the current lineup is stored; it is not an action button.

### Changes before lock

Tap a player to change the XI or match roles. The action changes to **Resubmit XI**. Review any transfer impact and confirm the resubmission before lock.

### Resubmitting an earlier fixture

If a previous match is resubmitted before it locks:

1. Later submitted lineups are reset.
2. Transfers and boosters charged to those later submissions are refunded.
3. The revised XI is carried forward.
4. The later fixtures must be reviewed and submitted again.

The confirmation warning lists this effect before the resubmission proceeds.

### Match lock

After a fixture locks, its XI can no longer be changed. If a later fixture is blocked, an earlier open fixture may need a submitted XI first.

## Boosters

- **3X** — triples one selected player's impact for the match.
- **2UP** — doubles the final match total.
- **SUP-TR** — enables unlimited transfers for that match.

Availability and usage limits depend on league rules. Reset later submissions refund boosters attached to those reset lineups.

## Privacy, scoring, and results

- Other owners' lineups remain hidden before lock when lineup privacy is enabled.
- **Fixtures** shows whether a match is upcoming, locked, or completed.
- **Results** shows submitted teams and scoring details after results are published.
- **Ranking** updates from published match points and can be filtered by phase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is SAVED not tappable?

SAVED is a confirmation status. Edit a player or role to enable **Resubmit XI**.

### Why does my transfer balance differ from the raw player changes?

Some changes may be free under league rules. For example, selecting your own player is not charged, and the first submission in a period can be configured as free.

### When can other owners see my team?

When lineup privacy is enabled, they cannot see it until the fixture locks.

### Why can I not submit a later match?

An earlier open fixture may require a submitted XI first. Follow the prompt to open that prior match, submit it, and then return.

### Which rules should I rely on?

Use the values displayed in the active **League** sheet and **Rules** page. They take priority over examples in this guide.
